# Templates: the install matrix

One row per template: which decision gates it, and where it lands. Your AI reads this during Phase 2 of `IMPLEMENT.md`; it does not act on its own.

| Template | Gated by | Lands at |
|---|---|---|
| `skills-index.md` | DP-6, option B only (no index exists, and there are five or more skills) | Your workspace, wherever you keep a list of your skills; the exact path is recorded in `STATUS.md` |

The plan card and the cold-read fixture are not here: they ship inside the skill, at `skill/templates/`, and arrive with it in Phase 2 step 1.
